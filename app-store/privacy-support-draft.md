# Beetle Swarm — Privacy & Support

Draft for publication with the iPhone release. Last updated: 6 September 2026.

## Privacy

Beetle Swarm runs on your iPhone. No account, adverts, tracking or analytics are included. The game does not send personal information to us. Your current voyage stays in memory and ends when the app is closed or restarted.

If you email for help, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to reply and resolve your request. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence. Apple may process App Store and device information under its own privacy policy. This policy describes the standalone iPhone game; any separately hosted website may involve its hosting provider's processing of web requests.

Support and privacy contact: charlie@cm95.co.uk. Confirm this as the public contact before publication and add the legal publisher's name and contact information where applicable.

## How to play

Tap Set Sail. Swipe up, down, left or right to steer. Leave safe land to draw a trail, then reconnect with safe land to capture territory. Protect your unfinished trail from beetles and avoid crossing it yourself. Capture 80% to reach the next chapter. Each new chapter adds one beetle. You begin a voyage with three lives.

Use the pause button to stop and resume. Switching apps pauses active play. Closing or restarting the app begins a new voyage.

For a problem with the game, email the contact above with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and what happened. Screenshots can help, but do not include passwords or sensitive information.
